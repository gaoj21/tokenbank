A planner writes a plan, an executor answers, a reviewer accepts or rejects, for up to five rounds. Each role adds calls, and on open-book tasks every call re-reads the documents.

2

The pipeline we study is a standard plan, execute, review team. It costs 1.3 to 6.3 times a single agent at the same reasoning setting. Between 28 and 57 percent of the cost is coordination: the planner and reviewer calls themselves.
