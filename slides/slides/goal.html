The problem

Minimise cost, subject to a certified bound on the accuracy lost

min J(π) s.t. Pr( R(π̂) > ε ) ≤ δ

J: expected tokens per task.

R: accuracy lost relative to always running the team.

ε: the user’s tolerance, e.g. 5 points. δ = 10%.

6

We turn the question into a constrained problem. Minimise expected cost, subject to a bound epsilon on the accuracy lost relative to the team. Since the policy is fitted on a finite calibration sample, the bound holds with probability at least one minus delta over that sample. The constraint is always feasible: always running the team has zero risk.
