Whether the second rung pays for itself depends on the model and the task, so CLIMB lets calibration choose. It certifies two one-decision families, each at level delta over two, and deploys the cheaper. If no cheaper policy passes, it runs the full team, so the worst case is no savings. The guarantee needs neither the monotone condition nor a calibrated score, only i.i.d. calibration and deployment tasks, including the model's outputs. It bounds the loss relative to the team, not to the best possible system.
