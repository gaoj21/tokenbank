CLIMB

When to Call the Team

Risk-controlled escalation for multi-agent LLM pipelines

Jianjun Gao, Jinxin Liu, Dongyun Lin, Hongyun Cai, Zheng Ma

Multi-agent pipelines can be more accurate than a single model call, but they cost several times as much. This talk asks when the team is worth its cost, and how to skip it without giving up more than a user-set amount of accuracy, with a guarantee.
