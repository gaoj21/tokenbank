Replay protocol

Record every rung and the team on every task. Draw 200 random 50/50 calibration and evaluation splits.

Violation rate: share of splits where the deployed policy loses more than ε on the full pool.

12

MATH is scored by symbolic equivalence, BBEH by its official fuzzy match, FRAMES and MuSiQue by an LLM judge. FRAMES and MuSiQue are open-book: every call receives the documents, median 17k and 2.4k tokens, so every rung pays for them. Because we record every rung's outcome for every task, we can replay any policy on any split. Tolerances are absolute: epsilon equal to 10 percent allows a loss of 10 accuracy points.
