Method

The loss is signed: skipping the team can also keep answers

ℓ = Y(team) − Y(answer kept) ∈ {−1, 0, +1}

+1

Stopping loses: the cheap answer is wrong, the team’s is right.

0

No change: both right or both wrong. Most stopped tasks.

−1

Stopping saves: the cheap answer is right, the team’s is wrong.

Clipping the loss at zero ignores the answers stopping saves. Since most losses are zero, a test that adapts to the variance can certify much more.

8

Because the team sometimes loses answers that a cheaper rung gets right, the per-task loss of stopping is signed. Keeping the sign matters where the team often loses correct answers. And because the loss is zero on most tasks, its variance is small, which a variance-adaptive test can exploit. Both points come back in the results.
