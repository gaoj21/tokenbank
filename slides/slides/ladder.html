Method

CLIMB: a ladder of cheaper configurations of the same model

L₀, direct answer at the cheapest reasoning setting, greedy.

L₁, single agent at the team’s own setting, from scratch, without L₀’s answer.

Team: planner, executor, reviewer, up to 5 rounds. The reference.

Scores: S₀ = L₀’s mean token log-probability. S₁ = 10·1[a₀ = a₁] + s̄₁, so agreeing tasks rank first.

7

A task climbs a ladder. The direct answer is the cheapest rung. The single agent is the team's executor without a plan or a review. Calibration chooses one depth: stop at the direct answer when it is confident, or run both cheap rungs and stop at the single agent, agreeing tasks first. Every other task runs the full team from scratch. Both scores are free: log-probabilities come with the call, and agreement needs no extra call because L1 never sees L0's answer.
