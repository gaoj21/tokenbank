The net gain over a single agent, 0.6 to 9.4 points, is the difference of two larger flows. The team rescues answers but also loses some. Most losses come from a wrong first draft that the reviewer accepts: the reviewer accepts 23 to 58 percent of incorrect answers. The losses are not just noise: rerunning the team on 300 tasks per setting reproduces 66 of the 142 losses against the direct answer.
