Every row here is a valid test of the same policy family, so the difference is only in efficiency. Mapped to zero to one, the signed losses sit at one half, and Hoeffding-Bentkus, which assumes the worst variance at that mean, certifies nothing. Clipping the loss to the harm indicator ignores the answers stopping saves. The exact binomial test of the clipped loss wins only on three settings, two of them on MATH, where the team rarely loses correct answers. Bonferroni in place of sequential testing loses most of the rest.
