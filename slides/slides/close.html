Takeaways

Call the team only when a cheaper answer is not certified

Teams are not uniformly better

They cost several times a single agent and sometimes lose its correct answers.

Signed loss, adaptive test

A betting test in fixed sequence certifies the cheapest safe policy, with the team as fallback.

Savings within the bound

38% of tokens saved at 5 points, 65% at 10, with failure rates at most 3.4%.

Scope: the guarantee assumes calibration and deployment share one distribution, so thresholds are best calibrated on the target tasks. Recalibration takes under a second once records exist.

To summarise: whether to pay for the team is a constrained decision with a signed loss. Under a monotone-gain condition a confidence threshold is optimal, and CLIMB certifies that family with a variance-adaptive betting test whose bound holds, given i.i.d. data, whether or not the condition does. Where confidence misleads, it saves little rather than losing accuracy. Weighted or online calibration could extend the guarantee to shifting distributions.
